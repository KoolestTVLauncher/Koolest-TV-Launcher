# Changelog

All notable changes to Koolest TV Launcher. The full notes for each version are on the [Releases](https://github.com/KoolestTVLauncher/Koolest-TV-Launcher/releases) page. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [Unreleased]

- The remote's Menu button opens the app menu, the same one as holding OK.
- All apps has an app menu too: put first in row, add to the menu, App info, Uninstall.
- Uninstall in the home row's app menu.
- Remote buttons (Settings → Remote buttons): Koolest learns any remote's buttons when you press them and lets you choose what each one does (open an app, All apps, Search, Koolest settings, screensaver, Android settings, nothing), only in Koolest or everywhere. Unnamed buttons are named when added; Reset all buttons undoes everything.
- Settings → Continue Watching → Shows per app: 5, 10 or 15 shows from each app (#18).

## [2.5.1] - 2026-10-27

- App names follow Koolest's language, from each app's own translations.
- Input tiles show the names you gave inputs in the TV's settings; plain ports use the language's digits.
- Persian uses Persian letters only, with no hamza.

## [2.5.0] - 2026-10-27

- Added French and Persian (right to left, Persian digits) in Settings → Look → Language.
- The Home button now covers whichever home screen the TV ships, not only Google TV's. Phone apps installed on the TV show in the apps row.
- Default home says Yes only when Home really opens Koolest.
- Koolest starts on your first app.
- Fixed Back on Android 16, the daily update check jumping to the Updates row, and crashes when opening an uninstalled app or a missing settings page.

## [2.4.0] - 2026-10-26

- Now playing and the Persian (Shamsi) date in the top bar.
- Reorder or hide rows; screensaver timing; both calendars in one tile.
- The glass shows what is really behind it, and items fade as they slide under the menu.
- Fixed the background stretching between items and the focus light sweep stopping short.

## [2.3.0] - 2026-10-26

- Liquid Glass design: real glass on the top bar and menu, settings as a floating card, and a sweep of light on focus.
- Softer light mode.

## [2.2.0] - 2026-10-25

- New screensaver with a glass clock, calendars, weather and Now Playing; 21 paintings.
- Unsplash photos are chosen to suit the theme.

## [2.1.1] - 2026-10-25

- **Keep Home working** and **Only launcher** connect only when the TV says debugging is on.
- Koolest stops if the debugging connection sends something unexpected.

## [2.1.0] - 2026-10-25

- Added **Keep Home working** and **Only launcher**, which do the adb setup from the remote.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-21

- New glass design, All apps with a segmented sort control, and Unsplash wallpapers.
